---
name: Shareholder notice versus statement
description: Why the Farm needs distinct pre-decision notices and retrospective statements, and plain report vocabulary.
---

The distribution notice is sent before a shareholder decides what to do with the distribution, before cash payment or an APIC direction. The statement records what was paid or retained afterward. Keep these separate documents even though their design is shared.

**Why:** The user wants a pre-decision notice without payment history or process explanations. Family readers do not know what “declared” means in this context; the user's watchword is “simple and clear.” The user also finds forced four-decimal share counts confusing and unnecessary, and wants consistent share presentation across the Farm tools, including the Roster.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction in future report work. Use plain distribution wording for shareholder-facing copy rather than financial approval jargon; do not add retrospective payment/retention details to the notice.
